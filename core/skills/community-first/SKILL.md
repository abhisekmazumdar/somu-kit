# Community First

In shared projects, the project's long-term health comes before one person's deadline.

- Solve it upstream when it's a general problem. Keep client-specific needs out of the shared code.
- Respect the maintainers' direction and the project's policies. Ask before a big change.
- Credit people by name for reports, reviews and ideas.
- Be specific and kind with newcomers. Leave the next step clear enough that they can do it.
